01 / SELECTED EXPERIENCES

Journalism

Behind the headlines: My journey through broadcasting, reporting and storytelling.

PHOTO 01 / PRESS CONFERENCE

Inside the Press Room

During my internship at the Korean National Assembly's broadcasting station, I had the opportunity to attend press conferences alongside professional reporters. I sat toward the back of the room, carefully listening to each statement and taking notes.

I wanted to learn how reporters turn political events into clear news stories. Later, I used what I had observed to practice writing evening news scripts and recording my own voice.

It was exciting to see journalism happening right in front of me, rather than just watching it on television.

PHOTO 02 / INTERNSHIP EXPERIENCE

A Month Behind the News

For one month, I worked at the National Assembly's broadcasting station from 10 a.m. to 8 p.m. each weekday.

It was a busy period in Korean politics following the martial law crisis and the impeachment of former President Yoon Suk Yeol. Breaking news often arrived unexpectedly, and I watched the newsroom make last-minute changes to scripts and cue cards before broadcasts.

Through this experience, I learned that journalism requires more than confidence in front of a camera. It takes preparation, accuracy and the ability to stay calm under pressure.

Every day made me more excited about my future as a journalist.
