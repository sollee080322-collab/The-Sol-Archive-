02 / SELECTED EXPERIENCES

School Activities

Finding my voice through school broadcasting, leadership and the Christmas Concert.

PHOTO 01 / THE REHEARSAL

Before the Spotlight

I was not a member of my school's media team, but I wanted to experience broadcasting for myself. Instead of waiting for an opportunity, I asked if I could help with the morning announcements. I began writing scripts and speaking on the school broadcast.

Over time, my teachers noticed how seriously I took each opportunity. When the school began preparing for its annual Christmas Concert, I was recommended to serve as one of the main MCs.

PHOTO 02 / BEHIND THE SCENES

Learning to Lead Behind the Stage

During rehearsals, I watched my classmates perform while the media team checked the cameras and sound. I worked with the team to review parts of the rehearsal that needed improvement.

I had always loved the idea of speaking on stage, but the rehearsals showed me another side of the experience. A successful concert depended on many people working together, including those the audience would never see.

PHOTO 03 / CONCERT DAY

Finding My Voice on Stage

On the day of the Christmas Concert, I stood on stage with a microphone in my hand. I was no longer practicing announcements in a classroom. I was speaking in front of students, teachers and families.

As I introduced each performance, I remembered how this opportunity had started with a simple request to join the morning broadcasts.

That experience taught me that I did not have to wait for someone to offer me a chance. Sometimes, I had to ask for one first.
